Tally Counter – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Tally Counter (“the app”) is a counter for Android phones and Wear OS watches. This policy explains what information the app uses and what choices you have.

In short

  • There are no accounts. I do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your counters and counts stay on your device. I never receive them.
  • The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The app asks Google Firebase once in a while if your version is still allowed. This is the only thing it sends.
  • The app asks for the internet permission, only for that version check.

Information that stays on your device

The names, colors, goals and counts of your counters, the time of each count, and settings such as vibration and sound. This information is stored in the app’s private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read it.

Backup files you create

You can export your counters and history to a file, or to a spreadsheet file (CSV). The app writes the file where you choose, through Android’s file picker. I do not receive the file. You decide where to keep it and who to share it with. You can also import a backup file that you made before.

Android system backup

Android can back up app data to your Google account if you turned on backup in your phone’s settings. If you did, your counters can be included. This is done by Android and Google, not by me. You can turn it off in Settings → Google → Backup.

Wear OS watch app

If you use the watch app, your phone and your watch send the counter names, values and your taps to each other through Google’s Wearable Data Layer. This stays between your own paired devices. I do not receive it.

Home screen widget

The widget shows one of your counters and lets you count from the home screen. It uses the same data on your device. It sends nothing anywhere.

Purchases

The Pro upgrade is bought through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment and your payment details. The app does not see your card or account details. The app asks Google Play only whether you own Pro, so it can unlock the Pro features.

Permissions

The app asks for the internet permission and the network state permission, only for the version check described below. It does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage permissions.

Version check (Firebase Remote Config)

The app uses Firebase Remote Config, a service of Google, to learn which app versions are still allowed. If your version is too old or has a serious problem, the app asks you to update. The service does not receive your counters, counts or any content of the app. Google receives a Firebase installation ID and basic device and app information (for example the app version, the device model and the Android version) to answer the request. Google handles this data under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. I use the answer only to show the update message.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). I do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example if it gets ads or analytics, I will update this page and the date above before that version is released.
