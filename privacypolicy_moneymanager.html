MoneyManager – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

MoneyManager (“the app”, package com.kiss.moneymanager) is a personal finance app for Android phones. This policy explains what information the app uses and what choices you have.

In short

  • Your money data stays on your phone. The app has no account and no server of mine.
  • I do not receive what you enter. I cannot see it.
  • The only things that leave your phone are what the ads and the crash reports need, described below.

What the app keeps, and where

The ledgers, accounts, categories, transactions, budgets, goals, notes and settings you enter are kept in a database on your phone, in the app’s private storage. Other apps cannot read it.

  • Backups. If you tap “Back up now”, the app makes one file with your data and hands it to you to save where you choose, such as your cloud drive. Where it goes from there is up to you. The file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere private.
  • Erasing. “Erase all my data” in your profile, or uninstalling the app, removes the data from your phone. A backup file you made is not removed.
  • Device backup. The app does not take part in Android’s automatic backup.

Advertising

The app shows a banner on its report screens. The ads come from Google AdMob. To show an ad, Google’s software on your phone sends Google information such as your device’s advertising ID, your approximate location (from your IP address) and technical details about the device and the app, as Google describes in its own policies. Google may use it to choose and measure ads. I do not receive this information, and I never give Google anything you entered in the app.

  • Your choice. Where the law requires it (in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, for example), the app asks for your consent before the first ad, with Google’s consent form. You can change your choice at any time in Settings, “Privacy choices”.
  • Your phone’s settings. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Google, Ads).
  • Google’s privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Crash reports and usage statistics

If you turn on usage statistics in your profile, the app sends anonymous usage figures and crash reports to Google Firebase. They are off until you turn them on, and you can turn them off again.

Children

The app is not meant for children under 13 (or the age set by the law where you live), and I do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Your rights

Because I hold none of your data, there is nothing for me to show, correct or delete. For what Google holds, use your Google account’s privacy controls. If you live where the law gives you more rights, write to me at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts doing something new with data, I will change this page first and say so in the app’s update notes.
