Rowly – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Rowly (“the app”) is a row and stitch counter for knitting, crochet and other crafts. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. The app is provided by Nuno Pontes (“I”, “me”).

In short

  • There are no accounts. I do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, location, or the names, notes and yarn you type.
  • Your projects, counters, notes, yarn and settings stay on your device only.
  • The app uses Google services for ads, anonymous analytics and crash reports. Ads and analytics follow your consent choice (asked first where the law requires it), which you can change at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Crash reports are sent when the app crashes.
  • Rowly Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. I never see your payment details.

Information that stays on your device

Everything you type into a project (project names, counters, row notes, repeats, hook sizes, yarn) and your settings (theme, vibration, sound, screen-on, volume keys, left-handed layout) are stored in the app's private storage on your device. I never receive them. Removing the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes them. If you turn on Android's backup, Google may store a copy of this data in your Google account under your own Google settings.

With Rowly Pro you can attach a pattern (a photo, a scan or a PDF) to a project. The app copies the file you pick into its private storage and shows it on the screen. The file never leaves your device. Pattern files are not part of the backup file.

When you choose “Save a backup”, the app writes a file to the place you pick. Where that file goes afterwards (for example a cloud drive) is your choice.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: used for ads, analytics, crash reports and purchases.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ads service, only according to your consent and your device's advertising settings.

The app does not use your camera, microphone, contacts or location. It reads a file only when you pick one: a pattern for a project, or a backup file to restore.

Consent and your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other regions that require it, the app shows Google's consent form on first launch. Analytics and ads are only used according to your answer. You can open the form again from Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your Android advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device's Google settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Rowly Pro removes the ads.
  • Debug builds used for development never send analytics or crash data.

Retention

Analytics and crash data are kept by Google for the periods configured in Firebase (analytics event data is kept for up to 14 months). I do not keep any other copy of personal data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Data sent to Google services travels over encrypted connections. Locally stored data is kept in the app's private storage.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or UK GDPR) you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. Because I cannot identify you from the anonymous data the app collects, the easiest way to exercise these rights is to withdraw consent in the app and reset your advertising ID. You can also contact me, and you may complain to your local data protection authority.
