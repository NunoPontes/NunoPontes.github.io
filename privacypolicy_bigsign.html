Privacy Policy – Big Sign

This privacy policy applies to the Big Sign app for Android devices (the "Application"), operated by the developer hereby referred to as the "Service Provider".

What stays on your device

The messages you write, your saved signs and playlists, your style choices, your theme and language choices, and any photo you pick as a sign background are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded to the Service Provider or to any server. The Application has no accounts and no sign-in.

If you choose "Share as image" or "Save to gallery", the image of your sign is handed to the app you choose or saved to your photo gallery. This happens only when you ask for it.

Information collected automatically

The Application and its third-party SDKs may collect the following information when you use it:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol address (approximate location derived from it)
  • Device and app information: device model, operating system version, app version, language, and crash reports (stack traces and the state of the device at the time of a crash)
  • Anonymous usage events, for example which features and options are used (the text of your messages is never included)
  • The advertising ID of your device, used to show and measure ads

How the information is used

  • Advertising: the Application shows banner ads from Google AdMob on screens other than the full-screen sign. Ads are never shown while a sign is displayed.
  • Analytics: anonymous usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase) help the Service Provider understand which features are useful. Analytics are switched on only where the consent choice you make in the app allows it.
  • Crash reports: Firebase Crashlytics collects crash and error reports so that problems can be fixed.
  • Updates: Firebase Remote Config and Google Play in-app updates are used to tell you when a new version is available or when an old version must be updated.

Consent and your privacy choices

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the Application asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before showing personalised ads or enabling analytics. You can review or change your answer at any time in the Application under Settings → Ads and privacy choices. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Cookies and tracking technologies

The Application or its third-party SDKs may use identifiers, SDKs and similar technologies to support advertising, analytics and crash reporting. Where required by applicable law, the Service Provider will obtain consent before using non-essential tracking technologies.

To request deletion of your personal data, to withdraw consent, or to exercise any of your rights, contact the Service Provider at nunopontes2015@gmail.com.

Data Retention Policy

  • Messages, signs, playlists and settings: kept on your device until you delete them or uninstall the Application. The Service Provider never receives them.
  • Automatically Collected Data (analytics and crash reports): retained for up to 24 months from collection, unless longer retention is required for legal compliance.
  • Aggregated and Anonymized Data: retained indefinitely as it no longer identifies you.

Photosensitivity notice

The Application can display flashing and strobing effects. Flashing lights can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy. Flash effects are off by default and are shown only when you turn them on.
