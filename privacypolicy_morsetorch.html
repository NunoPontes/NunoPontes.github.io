Morse Torch – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Morse Torch (“the app”) is a flashlight that can also blink text as Morse code. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. The app is provided by Nuno Pontes (“I”, “me”).

In short

  • There are no accounts, and I do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, location or the messages you type.
  • Messages you save and your settings stay on your device only.
  • The app uses Google services for anonymous analytics, crash reports and ads. Analytics and ads follow your consent choice (asked first where the law requires it), which you can change at any time in Settings → Privacy settings. Crash reports are sent when the app crashes.

Information that stays on your device

The text you type, your saved messages (with their speed and repeat setting), and settings such as theme, haptic feedback and whether you accepted the flashing-light notice are stored locally on your device. I never receive them. Removing the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes them.

Permissions

  • Flashlight: the app switches the camera flash on and off through Android’s torch API. It does not take photos or video and does not need the Camera permission.
  • Internet and network state: used for ads, analytics, crash reports and update checks.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ads service, only according to your consent and your device’s advertising settings.

Consent and your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other regions that require it, the app shows Google’s consent form on first launch. Analytics and ads are only used according to your answer. You can open the form again from Settings → Privacy settings.
  • You can reset or delete your Android advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device’s Google settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Debug builds used for development never send analytics or crash data.

Retention

Analytics and crash data are kept by Google for the periods configured in Firebase (analytics event data is kept for up to 14 months). I do not keep any other copy of personal data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Flashing light notice

Blinking light can affect people who are sensitive to flashing lights, including people with photosensitive epilepsy. The app shows a notice before the first transmission. Do not use the app if flashing light may harm you. Morse Torch is not a replacement for emergency services; in an emergency call your local emergency number.

Security

Data sent to Google services travels over encrypted connections. Locally stored data is kept in the app’s private storage.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or UK GDPR) you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. Because I cannot identify you from the anonymous data the app collects, the easiest way to exercise these rights is to withdraw consent in the app and reset your advertising ID. You can also contact me, and you may complain to your local data protection authority.
