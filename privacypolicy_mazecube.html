Maze Cube – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Maze Cube (“the app”) is a puzzle game in which you roll a ball through mazes on and inside a 3D cube. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. The app is provided by Nuno Pontes (“I”, “me”).

In short

  • There are no accounts, and I do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your settings and your game in progress stay on your device only.
  • The app uses Google services for anonymous analytics, crash reports and ads. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ads and privacy.

Information that stays on your device

Your settings (style, controls, ball speed, haptic feedback, line size, reduce motion) and the game you are playing are stored locally on your device. I never receive them. Removing the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes them.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: used for ads, analytics, crash reports and update checks.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ads service, only according to your consent and your device’s advertising settings.

The app does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location.

Ads

The app is free and supported by ads: a banner on the menu and settings screens, and a full-screen ad after every third finished game. Ads are never shown while you are moving the ball. The ads shown are chosen by Google AdMob.

Consent and your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other regions that require it, the app shows Google’s consent form on first launch. Analytics and ads are only used according to your answer. You can open the form again from Settings → Ads and privacy.
  • You can reset or delete your Android advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device’s Google settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Debug builds used for development never send analytics or crash data.

Retention

Analytics and crash data are kept by Google for the periods configured in Firebase (analytics event data is kept for up to 14 months). I do not keep any other copy of personal data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Data sent to Google services travels over encrypted connections. Locally stored data is kept in the app’s private storage.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or UK GDPR) you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. Because I cannot identify you from the anonymous data the app collects, the easiest way to exercise these rights is to withdraw consent in the app and reset your advertising ID. You can also contact me, and you may complain to your local data protection authority.
